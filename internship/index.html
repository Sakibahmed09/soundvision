Muslim Network TV Virtual Internships - Announcement

MUSLIM NETWORK TV VIRTUAL INTERNSHIPS - ANNOUNCEMENT

Online internships titles available for: Assistant Producer, Video Editor, Creative Writer, Production Assistant, Social Media Coordinator
Dates Needed: Ongoing

Muslim Network TV - Background

Muslim Network TV is a Satellite TV channel on Galaxy 19 covering the United States, Canada, and Mexico. Our programs can also be watched on MuslimNetwork.tv. We are already on Amazon TV, ROKU, and in a few days insha Allah, we will be on Apple TV and other platforms. So far we are producing eight hours of programming which is repeated three times in the 24-hour cycle.

Our team has 120 combined years of production experience. Our engineering team’s experience includes launching more than 30 television channels and digital media operations in the U.S. and abroad.

It is probably the only channel through which America can discover its Muslim neighbors. By bringing a variety of Muslim voices to the fore, this channel will fill in a gap in the national media landscape.

Sound Vision - Background

Muslim Network TV is owned and operated by Sound Vision, which is a 31-year-old not-for-profit 501 (C) 3 organization.

Sound Vision interns are important team members who learn and grow. Some have become managers and board members of Sound Vision. Others are serving at important positions in other media outlets and groups since we invest quality time in our interns’ development.

Sound Vision is the producer of Adam’s World, the Adam’s World Club and the Colors of Islam Club. We offer weekend Islamic school teachers training, Friday sermons, media training for Imams, spokesperson training, a free, 24-hour Crisis Text Line service. We have developed unique educational exhibits about the peace movement of Prophet Muhammad, peace and blessings be upon him, as well as the crisis in Kashmir. We have also led and continue to run several human rights campaigns of Justice For All, which is powered by Sound Vision.

Sound Vision is an intentionally diverse team. We thrive on diversity and would like that to be reflected in our team of interns. At this moment we have six interns working with us.

Internships Available

Please note: Due to COVID and physical distancing protocols, all of these internship positions are online and do not require presence on site.
Assistant Producer, Video Editor, Creative Writer, Production Assistant, Outreach Coordinator, Social Media Coordinator, Program Assistant
Assistant Producer will assist in the production of Muslim Network TV. S/he will be guided by experienced producers.

Video Editor will work under the supervision of three professional editors of Muslim Network TV.

Creative Writers will be involved in writing and reviewing scripts for different productions, including Adam’s World.

Production Assistants will assist with the regular production of the Adam's World Club and Colors of Islam Club, as well as all online education programming. Duties entail moderating live Zoom classes, ensuring classes are recorded and archived properly, and editing video recordings for future use.

Outreach Coordinator is needed to enhance the presence of Sound Vision as an Islamic educational resource in the DC/Maryland/Virginia area. Intern must have excellent oral and written communication skills. Duties will include research and social media work.

Social Media Coordinator is needed to develop marketing strategies for Adam's World App and videos. Duties will include developing affiliate relationships, publicizing projects, and creating news opportunities.

Program Assistant is needed to increase the use of supplemental resources for Adam's World videos and online classes. Intern should have some teaching experience and be able to develop creative materials that enhance learning opportunities for children.

Knowledge/ Skills/ Abilities:

  • Love for media and communication arts

  • Video editing

  • Creative writing skills

  • Social media marketing

  • Motivated to work alone and with co-workers

  • Customer service/relations

  • Phone/radio communications and etiquette

  • Ability to read, understand, and explain documents in English.

  • Speak clearly, concisely and effectively

  • Listen to and follow written and/or oral instructions

Internship Details

This position is currently unpaid, although a small stipend may become available depending on future funding decisions. However, the internship will provide excellent training for anyone considering a future in media and communication. We request a minimum commitment of 20 hours a week, with up to 40 hours a week available. Scheduling is flexible and could include a variety of weekdays as well as Saturdays and Sundays.

To Apply

Please send a cover letter explaining your background, experience and why you are interested in our internship position, along with a resume to: interns@SoundVision.com

Applications Are Due: Ongoing
