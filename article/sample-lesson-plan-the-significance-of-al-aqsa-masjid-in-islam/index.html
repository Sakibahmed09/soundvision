Sample Lesson Plan: The Significance of Al-Aqsa Masjid in Islam

From the archive · 2 min read

In this article
IntroductionInteractive LearningActivity

Duration: 60 minutes

Grades: 3-5

Objective: Students will understand the significance of Al-Aqsa Masjid in Islam, its historical and religious importance, and its connection to their beliefs.

Materials:

  • Interactive map of Palestine and Al-Aqsa Masjid
  • Pictures and videos of Al-Aqsa Masjid
  • Quran with relevant verses marked
  • Whiteboard and markers
  • Worksheets for reflection and discussion
  • Craft supplies for making a model of Al-Aqsa Masjid

Introduction

1. Opening Activity:

  • Begin with a short video showcasing the beauty and significance of the Al-Aqsa compound: Beautiful Adhan from Masallah al-Aqsa
  • Ask students if they recognize the different parts of the compound and what they know about it.

Interactive Learning

1. Interactive Map:

  • Use an interactive map to locate Palestine and Al-Aqsa Masjid.
  • Use an interactive resource to see the Al- Aqsa compound in 3D.
  • https://www.360tr.com/al-aqsa-mosque-front-right-panorama-virtual-tour_ef088987b6_en.html
  • https://www.360tr.com/the-dome-of-the-rock-square-1-panorama-virtual-tour_18352bb52b_en.html
  • Discuss the geographical location and its importance in the Islamic world.

2. Storytelling:

  • Narrate the story of the Prophet Muhammad’s, peace and blessings be upon him, night journey (Isra and Mi’raj) and his ascension to the heavens from Al-Aqsa Masjid. Here is a great resource: Isra and Miraj | SoundVision.com.
  • Highlight the significance of this event and its mention in the Quran (Surah Al-Isra, 17:1).

3. Discussion:

  • Engage students in a discussion about why Al-Aqsa Masjid is important to Muslims.
  • Ask questions like:
    • Why do you think Allah chose Al-Aqsa Masjid for the Prophet’s night journey?
    • How does knowing about Al-Aqsa Masjid connect you to your faith?

Activity

Craft Activity:

  • Provide craft supplies and guide students in making a simple model of Al-Aqsa Masjid. Here is a nice sample: Kid's craft activity - Light up Masjid al-Aqsa.
  • As they work, encourage them to discuss what they’ve learned and why it’s important.

Reflection:

  • What is the significance of Al-Aqsa Masjid in Islam?
  • How does learning about Al-Aqsa Masjid strengthen your belief?
  • Write a few sentences about what you learned today.
