Silat Al-Rahm: Importance of Maintaining Family Ties

By Umm Ahmed

Preserving the bonds of kinship is deemed an essential part of the Islamic faith. It is an obligation to be fulfilled without an eye for reciprocity. We are reminded to carry out this righteous duty with authentic references that can be found in the Quran and Sunnah. Here are eight ways to strengthen these family ties.

  1. Express love and respect for everyone.
  2. Honor your parents.
  3. Spend quality time as a family.
  4. Use technology to bridge the distance.
  5. Learn about your ancestry.
  6. Support and assist those who need help.
  7. Seek help from Allah when there are challenges.
  8. Convey rewards to the deceased.

Parenting Tip

Desmond Tutu had a wise saying: “You don’t chose your family. They are God’s gift to you, as you are to them.” Ask yourself this question: How might your life and our world be different if we truly treated our family members as blessed gifts?

Providing Support and Care for our Elders

By Sumayya Khan

Elderly parents and relatives are a huge blessing in our lives. As they age, it is likely that they will need additional assistance with everyday activities such as providing more direct physical, emotional and mental support. At that time, families need to work together to make decisions about living arrangements and expenses, medical care and treatment, caregiving, and more. Learn more about the details and about caregiving in general by reading the full article.

Hadith

Ibn 'Umar narrated, the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said:

"Nothing that a man spends on himself and his family, anticipating a reward from Allah, will fail to be rewarded by Allah Almighty. He should begin with those whose support is his responsibility. If there is something left over, he should spend it on his next nearest relative and then the next nearest.

(Al-Adab Al-Mufrad)

How to Make a Family Heritage Book

By Melissa Barreto

A Family Heritage Book is a simple, homemade book that houses a collection of items relating to your family’s members, history, culture, values, languages, and customs. This type of project is a wonderful way to start young children thinking about concepts of history and geography while also building their own sense of connection and identity within the family structure. Writer Melissa Barret provides beautiful photos from her son’s own cherished family heirloom and a lesson on how to start and complete the project.

Some Interesting Facts

According to the World Health Organization, people worldwide are living longer.

  • By 2030, 1 in 6 people in the world will be aged 60 years or over. At this time the share of the population aged 60 years and over will increase from 1 billion in 2020 to 1.4 billion.
  • By 2050, the world’s population of people aged 60 years and older will double (2.1 billion).
  • In 2050, 80% of older people will be living in low- and middle-income countries.
  • The number of persons aged 80 years or older is expected to triple between 2020 and 2050 to reach 426 million.

Encanto, a Celebration of Family: A Review from a Muslim Latina Perspective

By Wendy Díaz

Disney Animation Studios’ latest release, Encanto, is a story with valuable lessons that explores intergenerational trauma and how it shapes our families. As a Latina, writer Wendy Díaz appreciated the authenticity of the colorful characters and the thought-provoking, original soundtrack. As a Muslim, she welcomed the lessons about upholding family ties and celebrating each member’s unique role and talents. Check out more details and consider watching it with some popcorn as a family.
