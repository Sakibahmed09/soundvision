Helping Muslim Marriages Thrive and Grow

Muslim families are one of the most important stakeholders in the development of Muslim youth. A healthy marriage is crucial to the development of a positive and civic society.

95% Muslim youth in North America are neither in Muslim schools nor in Masjids. 75% Muslim youth say they or someone they know have been discriminated against. Muslim youth are the least happy and the most angry of all youth groups in America.

At the same time Muslims in North America are facing the highest divorce rate of any other Muslim community in the world. Our families are at risk.

As part of Sound Vision and Islamic Social Services Association (ISSA) USA's new strategic plan, we have launched a monthly e-newsletter, "Marriage Today ", to support and engage Muslim couples in the community to address the emerging challenges facing Muslim youth, Muslim families, and Islamic education.

This newsletter plans to address marriage from the premarital phase through the various phases of marriage to end of marriage by death or divorce.

Three Reasons to Sign Up for this Newsletter:

  1. Connect: Every spouse feels the need to connect, share ideas, speak their mind, and learn from other's mistakes. The Marriage Today newsletter allows you to hear other couple's voices and to have your voice heard.
  2. Grow: Many of us get married because we want to share our love and be beloved, and to enjoy a quality and peaceful time with the family. But we soon realize, due to tight work deadlines and unlimited list of chores, we are unable to grow and together in our relationship. The Marriage Today newsletter gets the right information and wisdom to your inbox, as we strive for quality and concision.
  3. Contribute: A healthy marriage is paramount to a family that gives back to the community. We hope that the Marriage Today newsletter will inspire you to grow our collective wisdom that can help to build lasting family institutions. We invite you to contribute to this newsletter with your contructive feedback and your marriage experiences.
