A Creative Memorial Competition For Chapel Hill Winners

In February 2015, three beautiful Muslim youth lost their lives in their hometown of Chapel Hill, North Carolina, due to the heinous acts of one individual. Deah, Yusor, and Razan were active in their communities and poured their hearts into the service they performed so graciously. They were loving people whose lives ended much too early, and they will be remembered, for their lives were not lived in vain.

These three young people were great Muslims, excellent citizens, and good neighbors. Today, Sound Vision Foundation challenges YOU (the students 13-18 years) to talk about their stories and lives in a creative way. Using your video camera or smartphone, you can tell their story or a story of any other inspirational Muslim; real or imaginary; of past, present, or future; on this planet or any other planet; depicting excellence in the area of being Muslim, citizen and a neighbor. Sky is the limit in terms of ideas.

Who can Participate?

Muslims and their neighbors of other faiths living in the US or Canada. Must be students (age 13-18 years).

Prizes:

First place: $1,500 cash prize

Second place: $600 cash prize (2 prizes)

Third place: $300 cash prize (3 prizes)

What Kind of Video to Produce?

A video based production featuring any of these genre:

  • Animated Typography
  • Kinetic typography
  • Spoken word
  • Songs
  • Animation
  • Documentary
  • Any mixture of the above

Competition Rules:

1. COMPETITION PERIOD.

Competition begins March 16, 2015 and ends 11:59 pm CDT May 31, 2015. All entries must be received by the end of the competition period.

2. ELIGIBILITY.

The Competition is open to Muslims and their neighbors of other faiths, ages 13-18 years living in the United States and Canada. The parent or legal guardian of a student below the age of majority in their jurisdiction of residence must formally submit the entry.

3. HOW TO ENTER.

In order to enter this Competition, entrants must:

-upload their video onto YouTube;

-read and agree to all rules and regulations;

-and submit the online entry form online.

4. THE ENTRY MUST:

  • include the title of an original video,

  • the filmmaker’s name,

  • have been created on or after March 16, 2015,

  • be three (3) minutes or less in length, including opening and/or closing credits,

  • only use authorized content, including, without limitation, music, images, film clips, and other intellectual property,

  • only contain images or likenesses of any individuals who have provided their authorization or whose parents or guardians have provided authorization if such individuals are under the age of majority in their jurisdiction of residence,

  • be appropriate for viewing by the general public (appropriateness will be determined by Sound Vision in its sole discretion),

  • be originally authored or the entrant must have written permission to use copyright protected material. All text, images, videos, and other content and materials submitted by entrants, including without limitation, the items described in subsections (A) – (H) above, shall be referred to hereafter as the “Submission.”

5. BEFORE YOU START:

  • No prior experience in film or editing is necessary. This film does not have to involve complicated technology, and can be created simply with the use of a smartphone and editing software that comes with most computers. (i.e. Windows Movie Maker, iMovie, etc.).

  • Shoot your video, and then fill out the application form. Once you are satisfied with your film, upload it to YouTube under the setting “unlisted”. Then copy the link into your application. This setting allows only people who have the link to view your video. The videos will be presented as anonymous to the judges.

  • Aside from assistance from a parent or guardian, these videos are to be directed individually.

  • You may have actors in your film if you so choose, but on your application, be sure to list their information in the designated spaces.

  • If your film is selected, it will be up to you to divide your prize money among your actors if they so desire. They will be judged mainly on content, but also on their presentability.

  • You may choose any style of film you wish, as long as it is creative and presentable. Any videos that are over the time limit or have obscene language or content will be disqualified.

  • Be sure to plan out your film before you begin shooting. You may need to sketch out scenes, dialogue, or the script for your voice over.

  • If you are stumped for ideas:

    1. you can make a video about you interviewing people in your community about their thoughts on the event;

    2. you may also choose to research the lives of these three individuals and do a voice-over which is placed over pictures that rotate throughout your video;

    3. you may also choose to film acts of community service and mix that with how Deah, Yusur, and Razan inspired you;

    4. you may choose to discuss the attention to intolerance this event brings into light and can discuss some past events and how they relate to the recent shooting, or even how hate crimes have affected you personally, or members of your community;

    5. you may even storyboard various scenes and make your film like a story with different actors who portray a certain message about this shooting, or hate crimes in general.

    6. you may use the Chapel Hill shooting as your inspiration, but feel free to branch out. Not every part of your film must relate to the event. It can simply be a basis.

  • When uploading, be sure that your file is compatible with YouTube, and that is uploaded in the best quality available off of your computer’s editing software.

  • Follow the instructions in the links below if you are having trouble uploading your video. If you run into technical issues that you are unable to resolve by the deadline, you may email us at talent@soundvision.com.

For any questions relating to the technology or the process of making the film, contact talent@soundvision.com.
