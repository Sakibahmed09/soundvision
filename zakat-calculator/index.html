Zakat Calculator

A clear place to begin.

Enter your assets, debts due now, and today’s gold or silver price. All values and results are in US dollars.

01 / Your thresholdSet your nisab

Choose the standard you use: 87.48 g of gold or 612.36 g of silver. Enter today’s price; this calculator does not supply live prices.

1 troy ounce = 31.1034768 g. Changing the price unit converts prices already entered. A price is needed for your nisab standard and for any metal entered by weight.

02 / Your assetsWhat you own

Enter the amounts you have determined to be zakatable. Leave unused fields blank; they count as zero. Include each asset once.

Gold and silver

Current value of gold, silver and other precious items. For each metal, enter its dollar value or its weight.

If applicable to your situation, enter the zakatable value.
