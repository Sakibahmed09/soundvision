Prophet Muhammad

  • Read and share the articles in this series, which Sound Vision is issuing today:
    • For individuals: What Can You Do When the Name Muhammad Is Mocked
    • For masjid leaders: Masjids Must Stand With the Name Muhammad
    • For interfaith partners: Can Interfaith Partners Please Stand Up Against Hate?
    • For families: "'They Laughed at My Name': Talking With Your Children About This Kind of Hate"

Prophet Muhammad's life and legacy is a source of guidance for us all at all times. It is crucial to re-read his life beyond the chronological events of history, but as a source of guidance for positive and practical impact on our spirituality, career, health, family, and community.

God describes him with these words: "O Prophet, indeed We have sent you as a witness and a bringer of good tidings and a warner. And one who invites to Allah , by His permission, and an illuminating lamp" (Quran 33:45-46).

To us, the Prophet was an ideal husband, father, father-in-law, son-in-law, friend, humanitarian, teacher, counsellor, Imam, leader, strategist, diplomat, and above all, a follower and servant of God. May God's choicest peace and blessings be upon him.
